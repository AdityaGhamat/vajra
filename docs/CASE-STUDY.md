# VAJRA / Progress is a practice

## Problem

A premium neighbourhood strength club needs to communicate coaching quality without intimidating the people it wants to welcome. A visitor must understand the options and know what their first visit would feel like before making an enquiry.

## Audience

Busy Pune professionals, first-time gym visitors, and returning lifters. Their practical questions are about confidence, time, attention from a coach, cost, and what happens next.

## Creative research

Research date: 17 October 2026. Awwwards category/nominee listings were accessible; individual requested listing pages timed out or were inaccessible. Live homepage inspection was used for the principles below. No claim is made that all three carry a particular Awwwards award.

- **Marathons** — https://www.marathons.com/ . Inspected the live homepage: a strong story hierarchy, documentary sporting imagery, and understandable topic navigation. Reinterpretation: a programme index, numbered entries, and functional schedule information, without copying its composition or carousel.
- **Tracksmith** — https://www.tracksmith.com/ . Inspected live homepage imagery and content hierarchy, including journal and community material. Cookie/region overlays limited the initial visual view. Principle: treat everyday participation as a story. Reinterpretation: practical encouragement, training notes, and varied everyday bodies, without using its brand, retail interface, or assets.
- **Audo** — https://audocph.com/ . Inspected the live homepage after dismissing its currency/cookie interfaces: warm, spacious architectural imagery. Reinterpretation: a calm photo essay explaining floor, coaching area, equipment, and changing space, without using its commercial assets or promotional layout.

## Design decisions

1. **One visual idea:** an independent sports magazine meets a training journal. Chalk paper, deep ink, vermilion entries, and olive/concrete surfaces make the page feel considered and physical.
2. **Type with a job:** Barlow Condensed 700 carries short editorial headlines. DM Sans 400/600 carries reading and interfaces. System monospace gives small labels a logbook character without loading another family.
3. **Photography as explanation:** generated documentary concept scenes show guidance, manageable movement, ordinary people, and a believable space. Clear labels avoid implying real staff or facilities.
4. **A varied grid:** asymmetric hero, narrow first-session column, indexed programme rows, structured timetable, aligned membership columns, and offset photography create rhythm. No repeated generic card system is used.
5. **Two signature interactions:** selecting a training row changes its photograph and details; a small page-progress line and section marker connect showing up, practice, and repetition. Native scrolling remains intact.
6. **One main decision:** “Book your first session” is the repeated primary action. Programme exploration and timetable browsing support it.

## Booking journey

Home → learn what the first visit involves → compare programmes → choose a timetable session → carry programme and session into enquiry → enter minimal contact preferences → receive useful validation → see an honest demo completion.

An equally short route is available from the hero and the end of every programme detail page. People unsure where to start can choose “Help me choose.” The flow clearly distinguishes an enquiry from a confirmed session.

## Portfolio narrative outline

- Lead with the hero plus a 360px companion view.
- Explain the beginner’s uncertainty and the club’s premium but approachable position.
- Show the typography/palette and how the journal vocabulary stays consistent.
- Demonstrate training selection and timetable filtering.
- Show one preserved-session enquiry, an accessible validation state, and the demo-complete message.
- Close with verified technical decisions and an honest next step: real photography, real operations data, a production enquiry adapter, user testing, and field measurement.
